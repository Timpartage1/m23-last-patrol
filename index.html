A STORY FROM EASTERN CONGO

THE LAST PATROL

A short 2D prototype inspired by the courage of Colonel Mamadou Ndala and the civilians caught in war.

Historical note

This is a fictionalized educational drama, not a documentary. Dialogue and missions are invented. Violence is stylized and non-graphic.

Overall experience

The diagnostic report includes browser type, screen size, language and saved mission—not your name, location or phone number.

How to play

Move and aim with the left control. Mamadou and his weapon face the last direction pressed; use OK to fire that way or interact with a nearby objective. Secure enough objectives to open restricted sectors.

Desktop: arrow keys or WASD to move, Enter or Space for OK.

JANUARY 2014

A soldier falls.
A story remains.

On 2 January 2014, Mamadou Ndala and two soldiers were killed when their convoy was attacked near Ngadi, north of Beni. A United Nations report described the assailants as unidentified. This game does not claim to resolve the disputed responsibility.

Dedicated to civilians and peacekeepers who have lost their lives in eastern Congo.
